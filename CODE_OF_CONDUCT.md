# Community code of conduct

Ble(e)p is built by people with different devices, studios, skills, languages,
and levels of protocol experience. Be curious, specific, and kind.

Expected behavior includes giving actionable feedback, separating evidence from
assumption, respecting safety and privacy, welcoming questions, and crediting
other people's research. Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination,
doxxing, deliberate publication of secrets, and unsafe pressure to test
hardware are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation
when needed to protect the community. Report conduct concerns through the same
private route described in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md); do not litigate private
or identifying details in a public issue.
