A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Can I buy a finished Ble(e)p?+

Ble(e)p is currently a DIY project. The firmware, printable enclosure files, parts list, and assembly instructions are available on GitHub. Start with the hardware guide to see what’s involved.

Will it work with my camera or lights?+

Support is specific to the model and control mode. Check the compatibility list for your exact equipment and the functions that have been tested. A brand name alone doesn’t guarantee compatibility.

Do I need my phone or the internet?+

Normal studio control and sequences run locally on Ble(e)p. A phone or computer can help with setup through its temporary Portal. Some integrations use your local Wi-Fi network; downloading firmware updates needs internet access.

Can one press start everything at once?+

One press can start an ordered sequence of supported actions, with waits where you want them. Steps run in order, so this isn’t frame-synchronized recording. You can review the generated Stop sequence or customize it for your setup.
